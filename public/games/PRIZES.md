# MFP Prize Wheel — Prize & Reward Ideas

Cheap and free prize ideas for the Master Food Preserver trivia table, organized
by age group. The goal is engagement, not expense — most players are thrilled by
a sticker and a "great job." Aim for a tiered system: **everyone who plays wins
something free**, correct answers earn an upgrade.

> **Budget rule of thumb:** plan ~$0.10–0.50 per player. A $25–40 prize kit can
> easily cover a full market day. Buy in bulk; reuse non-consumables (spinner,
> signage) every event.

---

## 🟢 Universal "everyone wins" freebies (all ages)

Hand these to every player so no one walks away empty-handed:

- **Printable certificates** — "Junior Food Preserver" / "Food Safety Champion."
- **Bookmarks** with a food-safety tip (e.g. "When in doubt, throw it out").
- **MFP / OSU Extension stickers** (bulk ~$0.05–0.10 each).
- **A fun fact card** — the fun fact from the question they answered.

---

## 👶 Kids (under 10)

Sensory, colorful, instant-gratification rewards.

**Free / printable**
- Coloring pages: fruits & veggies, "a day at the cannery," a happy jar mascot.
- Sticker chart / "I learned about food safety!" badge to color.
- Temporary-tattoo-style printable (strawberry, pickle, sun for drying).
- Connect-the-dots / maze placemats with a preservation theme.

**Under $1**
- Foam or vinyl stickers, scratch-and-sniff stickers (fruit scents fit perfectly).
- Pencils / mini crayons.
- Seed packets (radish, lettuce, sunflower — fast, kid-friendly growers).
- Temporary tattoos.

**Under $5**
- Small reusable produce/snack bag.
- Mini wooden spoon or cookie cutter (fruit/veggie shapes).
- A single-serving fruit-leather or dried-apple snack (ties to dehydrating).

**Engagement ideas**
- Let them spin the wheel themselves — the spin *is* the reward.
- "Guess the smell" jar (cinnamon, vanilla, dried herb) for a bonus sticker.
- Stamp a "passport" each visit to the booth.

---

## 🧒 Tweens (10–14)

Slightly more "collectible" and useful; they like earning things.

**Free / printable**
- Recipe cards: no-cook freezer jam, refrigerator pickles, fruit leather.
- "Food Preservation Methods" mini-poster or fold-up zine.
- QR card linking to the digital wheel or OSU Extension kids' resources.

**Under $1**
- Cool/holographic stickers, button pins.
- Seed packets (a "salsa garden" combo: tomato, pepper, cilantro).
- Pencils, erasers shaped like food.

**Under $5**
- Recipe card set on a ring.
- Small silicone jar lid / stretch lid.
- Reusable straw or snack container.

**Engagement ideas**
- "Beat the volunteer" — answer a harder card for a bonus prize.
- Take-home challenge card: "Make refrigerator pickles, bring a photo next week."

---

## 🧑 Teens (14–18)

Practical, "starter kitchen" items and things they'd actually use.

**Free / printable**
- One-page tested recipes (salsa, quick pickles, freezer jam) with the science
  callouts ("why lemon juice?").
- "Is it safe?" decision flowchart for canning acid vs. low-acid foods.
- QR card to OSU Extension / NCHFP resources and the digital wheel.

**Under $1**
- Seed packets, stickers for water bottles/laptops.
- Magnet with headspace / altitude quick-reference.

**Under $5**
- Canning funnel or jar lifter (single tool).
- Pack of canning lids.
- Reusable silicone food bag.

**Engagement ideas**
- Hardest-question challenge for a "real" canning tool (funnel, magnetic lid
  lifter).
- Invite to a teen canning workshop / 4-H food preservation project.

---

## 🧑‍🌾 Adults

Lean into information and credibility — the prize is often the *knowledge* plus a
useful handout.

**Free / printable**
- Tested recipe handouts (OSU/PNW/NCHFP): salsa, tomatoes, pickles, jam.
- Altitude-adjustment & processing-time quick-reference sheets.
- "Dial-gauge testing" reminder card (free OSU Extension service).
- Resource list: OSU Extension food preservation hotline, NCHFP, So Easy to
  Preserve, the MFP Volunteer Resource Hub app.
- QR codes to the publications behind each question.

**Under $1**
- Magnet or laminated quick-reference card (headspace, altitude, pH).
- Seed packets for a canning garden.

**Under $5**
- Single canning tool (funnel, jar lifter, bubble remover, lids).
- Pack of new canning lids.
- Printed mini-booklet of tested recipes.

**Engagement ideas**
- Sign-ups: MFP volunteer info, upcoming classes, dial-gauge testing dates.
- "Stump the preserver" — adults ask *you* a question; great conversation starter.
- Raffle entry (free) for a bigger door prize (a canning kit, a copy of *So Easy
  to Preserve*) drawn at end of day.

## Sourcing & logistics

- **Bulk stickers / pencils / tattoos:** party-supply and classroom-reward
  retailers; ~$0.05–0.15 each in packs of 100+.
- **Seed packets:** end-of-season clearance is very cheap; check with local
  nurseries or seed libraries for donations.
- **Canning tools / lids:** buy a multipack and break it up; hardware and grocery
  stores stock them seasonally.
- **Printables:** print certificates/coloring/recipe cards on the office printer;
  card stock for the nicer pieces.
- **Donations:** local hardware stores, nurseries, and grocers will often donate
  small items or gift cards for a community-education table — just ask.

## Suggested booth flow

1. Player spins the wheel → lands on a topic.
2. Volunteer picks the player's age group → reads a card (or uses the tablet).
3. **Everyone** gets a freebie (sticker + fun-fact card).
4. **Correct answer** → upgrade (seed packet / tool / better sticker).
5. Adults → handout + sign-up sheet + raffle entry.
